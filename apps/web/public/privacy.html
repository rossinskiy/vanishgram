Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026. This is an early version for a project under development.

What we cannot see

Message text, attachments and your recovery phrase are encrypted or kept on your device. The server stores only ciphertext that is waiting for delivery. It has no key to read it.

What the server does see

  • Your account ID and public keys, and your usernames.
  • Who sends a message to whom, when, and the padded size of the encrypted data.
  • Your IP address while you are connected (the hosting provider and Cloudflare also see it), and the time of your last activity.

What we do not collect

No phone number, no email address, no real name, no government ID. No advertising or analytics trackers. No tracking cookies.

Data on your computer

Your keys and chats are stored encrypted on your computer, inside the desktop app's local storage, under a passphrase only you know. Your theme choice is kept there too. If you forget the passphrase, sign in again with your recovery phrase.

Retention and deletion

  • An encrypted message is removed from the server as soon as your device confirms receipt, or when the sender deletes it for everyone.
  • Inactive accounts are deleted automatically after the period you choose, between 7 and 365 days (default 365). This cannot be turned off.
  • You can delete your account at any time in settings.

Limits of deletion

Deleting a message cannot remove screenshots, copies on someone else's device, or copies saved outside Vanishgram.

Security status

The encryption design follows established protocols but has not yet been independently audited. Do not rely on Vanishgram where your safety depends on it.
